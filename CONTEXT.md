# Personal Agent Operations

The language for coordinating Tom's coding and computer-work agents through one personal control surface.

## Language

**Goal**:
An outcome Tom asks the system to achieve, potentially through several coordinated pieces of work.
_Avoid_: Prompt, chat request

**Chief of Staff**:
The single human-facing coordinator that keeps work moving and brings Tom only difficult, consequential, or risky decisions.
_Avoid_: Chatbot, worker, team lead

**Worker**:
A persistent specialist assigned bounded work in service of a Goal, with its own identity, conversation, knowledge and responsibilities. Workers appear as Bots in the interface; the Chief of Staff owns the overall report to Tom.
_Avoid_: Harness, temporary subagent

**Escalation**:
A decision that requires Tom because the available choices are difficult, consequential, risky, or materially change the Goal.
_Avoid_: Clarification, notification, status update
